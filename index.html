Process

From first walkthrough to handover.

Every project runs the same five stages, whether it's one room or a fifty house estate.

01

Consultation and walkthrough

We visit the house or the plans, understand how the household actually lives, and note what's already wired.

02

System design and quote

A room by room automation plan and a fixed quote, tiered against Essential, Comfort or Signature.

03

Licensed installation

Certified technicians run and terminate every circuit, using hardware sourced through our official regional reseller.

04

Programming and handover

Scenes, schedules and access are configured on site, then walked through with everyone who lives there.

05

Ongoing support

A direct line for adjustments, plus an annual service visit under Comfort and Signature.
